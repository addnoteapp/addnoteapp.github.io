AddNote – Notes & Notepad

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This page applies to the AddNote Android application.

Who we are

AddNote – Notes & Notepad is an Android note-taking and productivity app. This policy explains how information is handled by the app and its third-party services.

Notes and local storage

AddNote is designed to let you create and manage notes on your device without requiring an account. Notes, tasks, and associated content are stored locally on your device unless a feature explicitly tells you otherwise. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may remove locally stored content.

Advertising

The free version may display advertisements supplied through Google AdMob. Advertising services may process device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID when available), ad interactions, approximate location inferred from IP address, and diagnostic information, subject to permissions, consent, and provider policies. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your region, consent choices, and applicable settings.

Learn more at Google Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions and purchases

AddNote may offer optional paid subscriptions through Google Play Billing, with RevenueCat assisting in entitlement and purchase management. The app does not need to collect your full payment card details to process a Google Play purchase. Google Play and RevenueCat may process purchase identifiers, subscription status, and related technical data. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions and reminders

AddNote may request permission for notifications or alarms when needed to provide reminder features. Permissions are used for the feature presented and can generally be managed in Android settings. Available permissions can vary by Android version.

Information sharing and retention

Locally stored note content is not intentionally provided to advertising providers as ad targeting content. Advertising, billing, and essential service providers may process data required for their functionality under their own privacy terms. Locally stored data remains until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall, subject to device backup behavior. Third-party retention periods are determined by the respective provider.

Your choices

You may edit or delete notes within the app, manage app permissions in Android settings, manage subscriptions in Google Play, and use any available consent/privacy options presented by AddNote. You can also review your Android advertising privacy settings.

Children

AddNote is a general productivity app and is not specifically directed to children. If you believe personal information involving a child requires attention, use the contact method below.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The revised date will be shown on this page. To contact the developer about privacy questions, use the developer contact details published on the AddNote Google Play listing.
